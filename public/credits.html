Data credits

Komorebi is a non-commercial student project. It does not own the titles, posters or descriptions it shows.

The Movie Database

Movies, series, cartoons, their posters and descriptions come from TMDB.

This website uses TMDB and the TMDB APIs but is not endorsed, certified, or otherwise approved by TMDB.

Film and TV news

Headlines on the home page come from the public RSS feeds of:

  • Variety
  • Deadline
  • The Hollywood Reporter
  • Collider

Komorebi shows only the headline, the source and the time, and every headline links to the original article.

How the data is kept

Komorebi saves a copy of each title someone opens or adds, so pages load fast and still work when TMDB is slow. Saved copies are refreshed regularly and never kept longer than TMDB's six-month limit.
