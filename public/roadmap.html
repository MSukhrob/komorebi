Next up

Connect your streaming accounts

Link Netflix, Disney+, Prime Video, Apple TV+, Max and other services, and Komorebi adds what you watch to your list by itself. No more typing in episodes by hand.

  • Netflix
  • Disney+
  • Prime Video
  • Apple TV+
  • Max
  • Hulu

Most streaming services have no public API for viewing history, so the plan starts with what is possible today: importing the viewing-history file you can download from the service (Netflix offers one), then a browser extension you switch on yourself, and official partner connections where a service allows them. You stay in control and can disconnect at any time.

  1. ConnectChoose a service and give your permission.
  2. MatchKomorebi finds each title in the catalogue and picks the right season and episode.
  3. ReviewYou check the suggestions before anything is added.
  4. Stay in syncNew viewing shows up on your list as Watching or Watched.
